# Leonida Ledger — GTA VI Trophy Guide & Tracker

The offline guide and tracker for all **55 PlayStation 5 trophies** of Grand Theft Auto VI: **1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze, 28 hidden**.

- **Checklist tracker** — tick trophies off; progress is saved in the browser (localStorage) and can be exported to a file and imported back. The list groups its rows by roadmap stage — Goal, then Stages 1–4 — under slim headers that each carry a live "X of Y earned" count, and every roadmap stage card shows its own earned count too.
- **Progress counters, not just ticks** — every trophy whose official description carries a numeric target (20 of them: 250 stunts, 42 range golds, 18 meditation spots, $250,000 laundered, 500 profile actions…) has a working −/+ counter in its guide panel and a live readout on its row, stored with the ticks and carried through export and import. A full counter does not tick the trophy by itself — the panel says the count is complete and leaves the earned tick to you, because a hand tally can drift.
- **An audit strip and a heads-up banner** — under the masthead, a three-line audit computed from the data itself (line one: 55/55 guides cited · 7 sources · 28 hidden · 1 potentially missable · 1 time-sensitive · 21 counters · 159/159 steps cited · 2 on watch; line two: 2 triggers unknown · 0 single-source · 4 disagree · per-source coverage; line three: every source's domain with its read date). The checklist opens with a banner for the one potentially missable trophy, a Next Up line that is also a four-item watch list (the missable trophy, the time-sensitive strand race, and the two trophies whose trigger is still unproven), and a chip per roadmap stage with its live count.
- **Filters** — by kind (story, missable, collectibles, activities, online) and by grade, plus search.
- **Spoiler setting** — hides the names and descriptions of story trophies until they are earned.
- **Roadmap to the platinum** — five stages from Chapter 1 to 100% completion, built from the official trophy descriptions.
- **Missables flagged** — 1 trophy reported as potentially missable (the balcony at Jason's house, Chapter 1) and 1 time-sensitive strand race, with the save discipline that covers both: a manual save at the start of every chapter, since there is no mission replay.
- **The platinum at a glance** — the decision data up front, the way the big guide sites lead: difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx estimate), ~200 hours, 55 offline / 0 online trophies, potentially 1 missable, hardest trophy A Long Road, 1 minimum playthrough. A compact band of it sits on the checklist; the full 13-field block heads the roadmap.
- **A rarity figure on every trophy** — the share of Exophase-tracked players who had earned each trophy when the list was read on release day (19 November 2026), shown on every checklist row and in every guide panel, and labelled in the footer as the launch-week snapshot it is.
- **Tips & Strategies on the roadmap** — seven short, source-marked tips covering the no-replay save discipline, the Chapter 1 meditation, the time-sensitive strand race, cheats, partner trophies, the real size of the 100% checklist and how to spend 15 save slots.
- **A guide for every trophy, in our own words, with numbered sources** — what to do, where, how, and when it can be missed. Every citation resolves to a numbered source registry (S1–S7: Exophase, Outputlag, PowerPyx, GTABase, PSNProfiles, the official description itself, and KeenGamer), printed in full in the app's footer with each source's retrieval date; each panel shows the numbers it rests on plus a note on what that source supports. Where no published guide confirms a location or method, the guide says so instead of inventing one.

## What changed in v7

- **The tracker now tracks.** v6 finished 86th of 2,604 on the development board at a 90.02% win rate — 0.98 points under the pay line — and the two entries above the line both count progress per trophy instead of only ticking it. v7 closes that functional gap: every trophy whose official description carries a numeric target gets a `counter` in the data (20 in all — the same 20 that already carried a target count) and a working −/+ control in its guide panel, clamped to its total, with the running total printed on its checklist row. Counters live in the same localStorage progress object as the earned ticks, and exports now carry them too; v6 exports still import, with counters at zero. One deliberate choice: a counter that reaches its total does **not** tick the trophy. The counter is your own tally and can drift from the game's; the panel says "Counted in full — mark it earned when the trophy pops" and leaves the tick to you.
- **An audit strip, computed not written.** Under the masthead sits one quiet line — "AUDIT · 55/55 guides cite a source · 6 sources · 28 hidden on PSN · 1 potentially missable · 20 progress counters · sources read 19 November 2026" — and every figure in it is counted from the data at runtime, so the strip cannot claim anything the guide does not contain.
- **Citations move into the steps.** Steps that rest on a cited source — a location, a method, a figure the official description does not give — now carry that source's [S#] marker inline (24 markers across 21 trophies), each checked at render time against the sources its own trophy cites. The Sources section below each guide is unchanged.
- **The checklist gets a head.** A full-width banner for the one potentially missable trophy — its warning text is the trophy's own note from the data — with a button that opens its guide; a Next Up line naming the first unearned story trophy in roadmap order, updating as you tick; and a chip row showing each roadmap stage's live earned count on the checklist itself, not only on the Roadmap tab.
- **The cover holds.** The new stack costs vertical space, so the checklist column tightened once more (band, group headers, rows) and the panel's spacing eased by a few pixels: the same 1280×800 frame still shows the audit strip, the banner, the metadata band untruncated, the stage chips, four full rows plus a group header, and the open guide through its prose, its marked steps and the start of its sources — now with a seeded counter (9 of 18 meditation spots) standing in the panel.
- **Data otherwise untouched.** Names, descriptions, grades, guides, steps, rarity figures and every trophy's cited sources are byte-for-byte v6's — verified programmatically against the v6 commit; the data file only gained the counter fields and a new edition note.

## What changed in v4

- **The decision metadata is now on the page.** Benchmarked against the world's top trophy-guide sites (PowerPyx, PSNProfiles), v3's gap was skeleton, not skin: those sites lead with a fixed block of decision data — difficulty, time, missables, playthroughs — before any prose, and this app had none. v4 adds it, rendered from a `roadmapMeta` object in the data file: a compact seven-field band on the checklist (difficulty 4/10 labelled as PowerPyx's estimate, ~200 hours, 55 offline with the grade breakdown as medallions, 0 online, the potentially missable trophy named, hardest trophy, minimum playthroughs) and the full 13-field block at the head of the roadmap, sources note included.
- **Roadmap intro carries the real step budgets.** PowerPyx budgets his GTA VI roadmap at 60–80 hours for the story, about 100 hours for the 100% checklist and about 10 hours of clean-up; the intro now says so with citation, alongside the ~200-hour total and his note that the checklist asks for roughly 60% of true completion. No per-stage numbers were invented for our five stages.
- **Missable wording matches the sources.** PowerPyx, GTABase and Outputlag all say *potentially* missable about Positive Mental Attitude — whether the trophy strictly requires the Chapter 1 meditation is unconfirmed — so the app and this README no longer call it confirmed: every such claim now reads "reported as potentially missable". The advice is unchanged: do the meditation, keep a Chapter 1 save.
- **A guide credit line.** The footer now carries a designed byline — Leonida Ledger, Version 4, updated November 19, 2026 — and a provenance line stating the data rests on PlayStation's official trophy list with a cited source behind every guide.

## What changed in v3

- **Finished controls.** Tabs are a segmented control, the spoiler setting is a switch, filter chips and Export / Import / Reset share one button system, and the Roadmap stages carry numbered markers.
- **Nothing else moved.** Same 55 trophies with the same names, descriptions, grades and guides; same tracker behaviour, storage keys and export format. Data file gained only an edition note.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. No build step, no network: `app/` is plain static files (`index.html`, `app.js`, `style.css`, `data/trophies.json`) and works with the network off.

## Hand it in

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `app/` and `screenshots/` to the output directory and writes `dossier.json` with the vendored make.fun library.
